# App Store Optimization (ASO) Documentation
**NavPath Academy - Maritime Exam Prep**

## 1. Audience Targeting
- **Primary Audience**: High school students (11th/12th grade) and recent graduates in India aiming to join the Merchant Navy via the IMU CET (Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test).
- **Secondary Audience**: Maritime enthusiasts, repeaters taking crash courses, and individuals looking for Diploma in Nautical Science (DNS) sponsorships.
- **Demographics**: Primarily male, aged 16-21.

## 2. Keyword Strategy
### Primary Keywords (High Volume, High Relevance)
- IMU CET preparation
- Merchant Navy exam
- DNS sponsorship
- Maritime academy
- IMU CET mock test

### Secondary Keywords (Long Tail)
- Merchant navy entrance exam prep
- IMU CET previous year question papers
- Nautical science diploma
- Join merchant navy India

## 3. Optimization Notes
- **App Icon**: Ensure the cyan/navy blue "N" logo stands out against white backgrounds in dark mode. High contrast attracts clicks.
- **Screenshots**: Highlight the 4-day streak (gamification) and the mock test UI. Use bold text on screenshots (e.g., "Take 120+ Mock Tests!").
- **Video Preview**: Show the smooth UI transitions (Cupertino page slides) and a user answering a mock test question and getting a result.
