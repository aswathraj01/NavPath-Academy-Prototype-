## Description
Chart your course to a career at sea with NavPath Academy! We provide a structured, exam-focused program covering Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and English tailored specifically for the IMU CET and DNS sponsorship exams.

**Features included in this version:**
- **Mock Tests**: Practice with realistic multiple-choice questions (e.g., Ship Stability, Kinematics) and get instant scoring.
- **Course Library**: Browse preparation modules like "IMU CET 2027 Complete Foundation" and "Merchant Navy Career Preparation."
- **Daily Streaks**: Stay motivated by maintaining your daily learning streak.
- **Study Materials**: Access downloadable PDFs and slides (Mocked for prototype).

*Note: This is an early prototype. Video playback and live payments are currently simulated.*
